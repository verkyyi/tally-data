## 2026-11-21 (fifth release)

- 22 listed software and AI companies from their filings with the US Securities and Exchange Commission, every reported quarter and
  year: CoreWeave, Pegasystems, GitLab, MongoDB, Elastic, Atlassian, Twilio, DigitalOcean, UiPath, Figma, Okta, Zscaler, HubSpot,
  Samsara, Dynatrace, Fastly, JFrog, SoundHound AI, Asana, Dropbox, Nutanix and Rubrik (revenue, net income, operating income,
  shares outstanding where reported).
- More open-source projects with stars and weekly npm and PyPI downloads (NumPy, Pydantic, Flask, pgvector, dbt, Prisma, Langflow
  and others). Star histories for React, Vue, Bun and Deno, which the last release lacked.
- Tally's own figures, kept like any other number: numbers tracked, dated values on record, daily visitors, visitors from Google,
  page views, and pages read by crawlers (https://tallycatalog.com/growth).

## 2026-11-21 (fourth release)

- Open roles for 37 more companies, counted from each company's own public job board (Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever) once a day, with
  the months the Internet Archive's copies of the board show: for example Scale AI from September 2022, Glean from December 2022,
  ElevenLabs and Cognition from March 2024. Boards that redirect to a company's own careers site have no archived past, and
  start now.
- Archived Greenhouse counts are corrected: a newer board shows fifty postings a page, so some past counts had stopped at 50.
  The count is now the total the page states, and a single team is no longer counted from the first page of several.
- More open-source projects with their stars and weekly downloads (PyTorch, TensorFlow, React, Vue, Svelte, Astro, Vite,
  Tailwind CSS and others; more are being added).
- A figure a source gives as "about" is recorded and labelled approximate.
- Anthropic is listed: its valuation is now its valuation at IPO, with the IPO price and shares sold.
- 598 numbers, 31,830 dated values.

## 2026-11-19 (third release)

- The histories of twelve of the most-read companies (Anthropic, OpenAI, Cursor, Perplexity, Mistral AI, Cohere, Databricks, Figma,
  Canva, Stripe, Hugging Face, Lovable) were checked point by point against the pages they cite: 317 values.
- About 140 values now carry the exact day and the page that first reported them, in place of a month and a second-hand page.
- 34 values were removed: running totals computed by adding rounds that no page states, reports of talks that had not closed,
  third parties' estimates, and duplicates. Five totals were corrected to the figure their own source states.
- A listed or acquired company no longer has a "latest valuation": Figma's is its valuation at IPO, and Cursor's and Hugging Face's are
  the price of the agreed acquisition.
- Two numbers left the record because nothing but a third party's estimate stood behind them.
- Every removal and change is listed with its reason at https://tallycatalog.com/corrections
- 538 numbers, 26,194 dated values.

## 2026-11-18 (second release)

- History is deeper: PyPI weekly downloads back to 2016 from the public download log, monthly archived counts for GitHub stars and
  job boards, more archived price points, and funding and valuation histories filled in by a second research pass.
- Values cited to a publisher's own estimates or market-implied figures were removed from the record (49 values across 18 numbers).
- Values whose source does not allow redistribution (company databases and aggregators) are left out of these files. The first
  release contained some of them; they should not be reused from it.
- Values that belong to a period or an event are filed under that date (a quarter's last day, the day of a round), not the day they were read.
- The licence statement now says what it covers: Tally's compilation, not the facts.
- 534 numbers, 26,102 dated values.
