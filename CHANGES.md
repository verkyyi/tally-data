# Changes

## 2026-11-18 (second release)

- History is deeper: PyPI weekly downloads back to 2016 from the public download log, monthly archived counts for GitHub stars and
  job boards, more archived price points, and funding and valuation histories filled in by a second research pass.
- Values cited to a publisher's own estimates or market-implied figures were removed from the record (49 values across 18 numbers).
- Values whose source does not allow redistribution (company databases and aggregators) are left out of these files. The first
  release contained some of them; they should not be reused from it.
- Values that belong to a period or an event are filed under that date (a quarter's last day, the day of a round), not the day they were read.
- The licence statement now says what it covers: Tally's compilation, not the facts.
- 534 numbers, 26,102 dated values.
